A kitten crosses three towns, PEACH, APPLE and LEMON, of six stages each: meadows, forests, waterfalls, lakes, bricks and cities, against the clock, jumping and throwing what it finds; at the end of each stage a balloon rises, and the last three spell CAT. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX point by point; the game in GIF, played in openMSX; the five tunes, from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1986 - 32 KB cartridge (header at 0x4000) - MSX - Dump 0039cff2...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM (checked against openMSX) and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the CASIO intro, the title, the controls, the jump and the throws, the time, the lives and the points.
  • The stages: the three towns, the six scenes, the screens, the objects, the balloons and the enemies.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the five tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: CAT in the balloons, SELECT and ESC on the title, STOP and F1, a demo played with tile drawings.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the cartridge, the BIOS it uses, the main loop, the screens, the RAM and the tools.
